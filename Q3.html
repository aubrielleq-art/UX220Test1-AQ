Current uses of the web

Tim's original purpose of creating the web, was so that scientists and researchers could easily share and display their findings, without having to constantly rely on sharing their paper notes and memos. Doing so often took much more time and resources. But with the existence of webpages, research became much more accessible for everyone. Nowadays the internet is still mainly used to display information, but also for so much more. People can display all sorts of media, information, and even art. Though in more recent times, the general consensus is that the internet is way too overrun with ads, often disrupting the viewing experience of users when they want to simply read or look at something on the internet.
